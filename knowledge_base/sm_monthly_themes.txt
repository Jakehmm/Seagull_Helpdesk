MONTHLY THEMES
The monthly themes are as follows: June is Love of School; July is Environmental Consciousness; August is Loyalty and Patriotism; September is Respect for Teachers; October is Diligence and Hard work; November is Cleanliness and Orderliness; December is Helpfulness and Cooperation; January is Politeness and Courtesy; February is Friendliness and Kindness; and March is Integrity and Honesty.
